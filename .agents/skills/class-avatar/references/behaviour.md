# How the class avatar behaves

Which pose shows when, how cues play, and where the avatar sits.

- **Which sessions:** a master whose class has a readable file (`TrackerAvatarCatalog.art(for:)`, kind `.master`), and a worker whose master's
  class has a familiar (kind `.familiar`, the worker's `class` is its master's). Shells and classes without art show none.
- **Poses:** `TrackerAvatarPose.pose(for:catalog:)` picks the steady pose, first match: blocked or needing input is calling out (the hooks block a session while its ask-the-user tool waits: Claude's `AskUserQuestion`, Codex's `request_user_input`, and Pi's `ask_user`; Codex's `request_user_input_async` returns at once and leaves the question open, so the session stays blocked through the Stop that ends its turn, with no bow, until the next prompt, unless a later tool in the turn moves it on); working with a tool is that tool's pose (search studies, edit inscribes, a shell command by its command, web scries, agent, plan and other tools spellsong); otherwise sub-agents running is spellsong, working is pondering, and the rest is idle. Stopped and errored sessions are idle. A shell command's pose comes from the one table in `TrackerShellCommand` (Core): a reading command (ls, cat, rg, git log, ...) studies, a writing one (sed -i, mv, cp, git commit, gofmt -w, and git's worktree, checkout, switch, merge and rebase) inscribes, a network one (curl, git push, every `gh` call, `questmaster pr`/`qm pr`) scries, a spawn (`questmaster spawn`, `qm spawn`) summons, and anything else (builds, tests, scripts) channels. The first program decides it, after leading setup segments (ended by `&&`, `;` or a line break: blank, a comment, `cd`, `export`, or only env assignments), env assignments and `rtk`. Codex's `exec_command` reads its command from the `Bash:` activity label, like the other agents' shell calls, and channels when it has no command text.
- **Familiars:** `TrackerAvatarPose.pose(for:catalog:turnUsedTool:)` picks a worker's pose with the familiar's rules, first match: blocked or
  needing input calls out; stopped and errored sessions idle; sub-agents running (whatever the status, so a done turn keeps working until they
  stop) is `working`; working with a tool in flight, or with a tool already used this turn, is `working`; working before the turn's first tool
  is `pondering`; the rest is idle. So a busy turn thinks until its first tool, works while the tool runs, and stays `working` in the gaps
  between tools until the turn ends. A familiar has no per-tool poses (any tool is `working`), sub-agent strum, summoning or cues, so its Core
  choice is one of five poses. The turn's memory of a tool (`turnUsedTool`) is kept by `TrackerAvatarDirector` (`noteToolUse`), one per attached
  session, so one per worker in the company. A cast, or sub-agents, seen while the turn is under way (working, blocked or needing input) sets
  it; a turn that ends (done, stopped or error) clears it, and calling out and idle don't. A master's pose ignores it. Known gap: a payload
  carries only the tool running when it is cut, and the daemon cuts a snapshot 75 ms after its last state change (`watchDebounceWindow`,
  `internal/serve/watch.go`). A tool that starts and ends inside that window is therefore seen only when the 1 s clock push lands during it, so
  a turn whose tools are all that short keeps thinking until a longer tool is seen. Attaching mid-turn with no tool running shows `pondering` too. The director is the same one as a master's, so the hold, enters, finishes, the bow at each
  turn's end and Reduce Motion behave alike. `TrackerAvatarEventDetector` raises events only for a master, and `TrackerAvatarDirector.answer`
  ignores any that reach a worker, so a familiar plays no cue.
- **Company:** an attached master shows its live workers' familiars in its slot: the workers that are children of the master and not stopped
  (`TrackerCompanyLayout.liveWorkers`, in the payload's order, which is spawn order). Each live worker has its own `TrackerAvatarDirector`,
  fed its own session, so each plays its worker's pose by the familiar rules, bubble included. A familiar's company comes in three sizes,
  1.5x, 1.25x and 1x of its designed scale (`company/1.5x`, `company/1.25x`, `company/1x`), each with its own pitch and full row (the
  designed row's span at the size's pitch and resting width, measured by the converter). The company draws at the largest size whose full
  row holds every worker (`TrackerCompanyLayout.sizeIndex`): 1.5x while the count fits its row, else 1.25x, else 1x; past the 1x full row,
  the 1x size with the `+n` marker. The size changes at once when a worker joins or leaves, with no animation. The Werebear's 1.25x row
  is its 1.5x row, so its company goes straight from 1.5x to 1x. The layout (`TrackerCompanyLayout.designLayout`) runs on the chosen size's
  row; a full row lands exactly on that size's designed places. The master never moves: its body stands at the slot's centre whatever the count. Its name tags' face box is its head box at the master's 1.5 pt pixel, so the tags keep off the face. The workers stand at the row's pitch, in
  spawn order from left to right, so the newest is always the right-most. The row's centre starts 20 pt right of the master's body
  column for one worker (`TrackerCompanyLayout.soloOffset`), and moves toward the full row's centre (the midpoint between its first place's
  visible left edge and its last place's visible right edge) in equal steps as workers are added: each worker added moves the centre by the
  same amount, and a full row lands exactly on its designed places (`designInset + i × pitch`, the first at 10 pt). A familiar draws from its
  `company/` frames at 0.5 pt a pixel (the 2x device grid), so each frame pixel lands on a device pixel. Each familiar in the company also
  draws a one-pixel outline in `AppPalette.familiarOutline` (black): the ring of transparent pixels round its figure, eight neighbours
  counted, painted into each figure frame when it decodes (`TrackerAvatarSprites.outlined`, `TrackerCompanyOutline.ring` in Core), so the
  figure separates from whatever stands behind it, frame by frame. A familiar's effect layers (`-front-` and `-behind-` frames) stay bare, so
  their sparks and specks get no dark edge. The worker slot's full frames and the master carry no outline. Each familiar's visible bottom sits 5 pt
  below the master's feet, and a hover's gap above its feet is taken out (`TrackerCompanyLayout.drop`). Past the row's capacity the rest
  are counted in a `+n` marker: a 20 pt portrait disc 5 pt in from the slot's right edge, with its chip centred above it. Familiars draw in
  front of the master, right-most on top. A worker whose familiar has no art
  takes no place. A worker attached (not its master) shows its own familiar alone, as before. The slot, banner and plates never move or
  resize for the company.
- **Entrance:** a live worker that joins the attached master's company while the master is shown, and gets a place on the row, plays its
  familiar's entrance (`TrackerCompanyEntrance`, Core). The familiars already on the row slide to their new places at once (`MotionStep.slide`,
  0.3 s, easing out, snapped to whole points), and the newcomer waits two ticks (`TrackerCompanyEntrance.entranceDelayTicks`, 0.1 s) for the gap
  to open. Then its entrance plays in place of its pose (`TrackerAvatarEntranceRig`, drawn from its company size's `entrance`, one tick each on
  `MotionTick`, 0.05 s), and its pose takes over where the entrance ends on the idle. Its name waits for the entrance. A size change moves the
  others to the new size at once and slides them. Nothing plays on the company's first sight, on a master switch (the baseline), under Reduce
  Motion, on a size change alone, for a departure (the row jumps, as before), for a worker counted under "+n" or one that moves into the row
  from "+n" (it was already live), or for a familiar without entrance art (it pops in). The entrance's timing is the authored 0.05 s tick, not
  `playbackSpeed`. Observing a company again changes nothing: the state is kept per worker id, and each tick re-places the row while a slide or
  an entrance plays.
- **Names:** a familiar in the master's company carries its worker's session title in a chip above its head while the worker is working (a busy turn, status working, whose familiar poses thinking or working) and the company has two or more familiars, and while the pointer is over the familiar (`TrackerCompanyNameTags.visibility`). A lone familiar's working name shows nowhere at rest; the pointer over it still shows it. The chip is 15 pt tall, JetBrains Mono 11 pt, on a 5 pt stem down to the head (`TrackerCompanyNameTags.place` in Core decides; `TrackerCompanyStage` measures and cuts, and the slot draws). Any other name shows nowhere and takes no room. The hovered name places first, so it always shows: while the pointer is over the familiar, the working names give way to it (a working name moves up a tier, is cut, or is left out) and come back when it leaves. The working names place after it, in spawn order. A tag appears and goes with the status or the pointer, with no colour change. The pointer's hit area is the familiar's resting box (`TrackerCompanyStage.HoverArea`: the figure's columns and opaque rows at its company scale). It changes no cursor and takes no click, and the "+n" workers have none. A hover clears when its familiar leaves the row. A name takes the lowest of three tiers with room over its head, 20 pt apart, keeping a 5 pt gap from its neighbours. A tier whose band runs into the master's face is blocked where the face is, from the class's `head` box in its `animation.json` (`TrackerAvatarAnimation.headBox`; every class has one, measured from its idle art). A name with no room on any tier is cut with an ellipsis to the widest free run over its head, and a name that cannot keep four letters and an ellipsis gets no chip. The "+n" workers and an attached worker's own familiar get no name.
- **Director:** `TrackerAvatarDirector` (Core) decides the pose, and it is pure. **Nothing that has an end is cut:** an enter, a finish and a one-shot (a release, a catch, a summoning cast's beat, the bow) always play to their end, whatever else is wanted. A loop has no end, so it can be left at any time: when another pose is wanted its own finish plays (if it has one), then the next pose opens on its enter. A steady change holds the shown pose for `TrackerAvatarTiming.holdSeconds` (1.0 s), counted from the pose's arrival (it stops a tool that flickers on for a moment from starting a whole enter and finish), and waits for the pose's enter to end if that is longer; only the latest wanted pose is kept meanwhile, so the poses wanted in between are dropped and nothing replays a backlog. Calling out follows the same rule but skips the hold and the queue: it waits only for the block on screen to end (an enter or a finish, about 0.5 s at most; the beat on screen, when a cue is playing), the loop it leaves plays its finish first, and it comes on screen before the cues queued behind. A steady change, unlike calling out, waits for every queued or playing cue to end first. Every turn the director saw working bows once at its end, as the turn's closing beat, after its sub-agents stop. A session that goes done without being seen working (attached already done) doesn't bow. Attaching to a done session, or switching to one, shows no bow. Tracker events (`TrackerAvatarEventDetector`, from consecutive payloads; the first payload, an attach and a switch are a baseline) play as cues, each one or two beats: a spawned worker summons (the summoning cast on its own beat, then the release; while a summoning from a running spawn command is on screen, the release waits for that summoning's finish), a message to a worker releases, and a worker's report catches as it arrives. Nothing flies: the cues are poses only. A cue never cuts an enter, a finish or the bow: it waits for it to end. A cue that leaves a loop leaves it through the loop's own finish (a loop with no finish block is cut at once), which plays before the cue's first beat, so the cue's beat starts when it ends; the figure rests on the finish's last frame until a beat covers it. The loop doesn't come back after the cues: when the queue is empty (or calling out is wanted), the latest wanted steady pose shows on its enter, the pose that finished included. A cue that comes while the master is calling out leaves calling out through its finish, and calling out returns on its enter if still wanted. At most `queueCapacity` (4) cues wait behind the one playing; a burst past that drops the newest. A release or a catch that names its `strike_frame` plays its entrance before its strike inside its own beat, which keeps the one-shot's length; the strike frame only times the figure's move (see `references/animation-file.md`). Under Reduce Motion the cues still play their poses.
- **Playback:** a loop cycles each track on its own clock, so a 1-frame character and an 8-frame front run independently. A one-shot, an enter or a finish plays each track once, holds its last frame, and lasts as long as its longest track (`TrackerAvatarBlock.seconds`). Each track is a discrete keyframe run on its layer's contents, and a discrete keyframe holds each value only up to the next key, so every sequence closes on a repeated key. `TrackerAvatarFrame.beat` rises whenever a pose's cue starts, so a repeated one-shot plays again. Changes snap: the layers take contents, position, bounds and hidden at once (`TrackerAvatarLayerActions`), so there is no crossfade. `testAvatarPoseSwitchesLandAtOnceWithNoImplicitAnimation` checks the keys after a switch. Frames have no glow or floaters.
- **Finish:** when the steady pose leaves for another steady pose, its own `finish` plays first (any pose with a loop may have one, a cast or not), once the hold has run, and the new pose shows when it ends, opening on its own enter if it has one. It is skipped for a switch to the same pose, when the pose has no finish block (it then snaps), and under Reduce Motion; calling out and a bow wait for the finish like any change. Nothing cuts a finish: a change that arrives during one doesn't restart it, a cue or calling out waits for it, and the pose wanted at its end shows, with the poses wanted in between dropped. The finishing pose itself, wanted back by then, opens on its enter again. The finish's layers hold the frame at its elapsed time (`TrackerAvatarFrame.finishElapsed`).
- **Enter:** a steady pose with a `loop` may open it with an `enter`. The enter plays once when the pose comes on screen (on attach, a switch, a change, or calling out), then the loop starts from its own first frame (`TrackerAvatarFrame.enterElapsed`; the view plays the tracks with `TrackerAvatarFrameRig.showEnter`). A change, a cue or calling out that comes during an enter waits for its end (a change also for the hold, whichever ends later); a pose leaving after its enter then plays its finish from its first frame. A pose that continues, a new turn or the same pose back from a cue, doesn't replay it. Reduce Motion skips it. A summoning cast's cue plays its enter the same way, on its beat, then one loop cycle and its finish.
- **Slot:** an attached plate reserves a fixed slot after the scrolling plate list, standing `TrackerListMetrics.avatarBannerGap` (10pt) above its session banner, which sits above the pinned shell section. Shell rows and no known attachment reserve neither slot nor banner. Its height is
  the tallest bundled class's idle figure in points at the master's 1.5 pt pixel, rounded up to 5pt (260pt now), and its width spans the plates and badge column
  (`TrackerListMetrics.columnWidth`). The avatar's sole stays
  on the slot's bottom edge for every pose, and that edge is `avatarBannerGap` above the banner's top (the gap is outside the slot); art below the feet shows in the gap. Effects are never clipped: they draw past the slot's sides, over the gap and onto the terminal beyond the column, and above the slot over the plates. The avatar takes no clicks, so the overflow passes them through to the terminal and the plates beneath. The banner covers art hanging below the gap, as before. A class change swaps the pose
  without changing the slot's size or moving the plate list or session banner.
- **Art decode:** a class's PNGs decode when a pose is first drawn, and that read costs a file open and a decode on the main thread. So each tracker update prefetches, off the main thread, the pose each session shows when switched to (`TrackerAvatarLibrary.prefetch`, one pose per session; a familiar's working or pondering comes from `TrackerAvatarDirector.turnUsedToolWhenSwitched`; a designed familiar also decodes that pose's company frames). A switch then draws cached frames and no decode. A switch that lands within the prefetch window (about a quarter second after the session appears) still decodes on the spot. The prefetch keeps what it decoded, so memory grows with the poses the tracker's sessions have shown. Other poses (cues, bows, calling out) decode on first use. A designed familiar's lowest resting row is read once (`restLowestRow`), as its columns are.
- **Reduce Motion:** the poses still change, but each layer shows the first frame of its track, with no frame animation, moves, breath, blink, fidgets, enters or finishes (`TrackerAvatar.updateMotion` stops them, and the director and fidgets skip theirs).
- **Constants:** `TrackerAvatarTiming` (Core) holds the pose hold. Block and frame times come from the animation file, fidget gaps from `TrackerAvatarFidgetTiming`, and drawing settings such as `pixelPoints` (1.5 pt, a class's pixel), `familiarPixelPoints` (1 pt, a familiar alone in the slot) and blink timing from `TrackerAvatarDesign` (App).
